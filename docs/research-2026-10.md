# What the sources say: audience retention, caption, hashtags, music, trial reels

As of 8 Oct 2026. Compiled by two research agents; the sources were opened, but some were read only as an abstract.
**Check the source yourself before quoting a figure.** Labels: **[D]** documented by the platform, **[B]** backed by data,
**[U]** unproven (rule of thumb or no evidence).

## 1. What makes people watch to the end

| Lever | Strength of evidence | Finding | For the edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| The first 3 seconds | medium | TikTok/Kantar, 3,500 ads, correlational: tension at the start +16% watch time [B] | start with the strongest image |
| Emotional peaks | medium to strong | Joy and surprise hold viewers (Teixeira et al. 2012, advertising) [B] | one clear peak, tied to the content |
| Face | medium, photos only | Instagram photos with a face: more likes and comments (Bakhshi et al. 2014); Li & Xie 2020 found no effect on Instagram [B] | face large and early |
| Text on screen | strong, for comprehension | Review of over 100 studies (Gernsbacher 2015) [B] | keep it |
| Vocals under the voice | medium, measured on reading | Meta-analysis, 65 studies: vocals disrupt more than instrumental music (Vasilev et al. 2018) [B] | music under the voice quieter or without vocals |
| Speaking pace | weak, lecture videos | faster and more engaged speech is watched longer (Guo et al. 2014) [B] | tighten pauses |
| Cutting pace | weak | more cuts, more arousal and memory (Lang et al. 2000, TV) [B] | every 2–3 seconds is enough |
| Saturation | not a lever | 7.6 million Flickr photos: saturation filters do almost nothing for views; warmth, contrast and exposure have a stronger effect (Bakhshi et al. 2015) [B] | match the clips, leave skin tones natural |

Not proven: an "ideal length", a "1.7-second window", "best colours" [U].
Measure by: share still watching after 3 seconds, share watching to the end, replays.

## 2. Caption and hashtags

- **Instagram:** at most 5 hashtags per post (since December 2025), targeted rather than generic [D, via trade press]. The
  caption is used for keyword search [D, 2020]. The platform has never shown that it steers reach in recommendations; the
  ranking explanation for Reels mentions neither caption nor hashtags [D].
- **TikTok:** 3 to 4 relevant hashtags, "more doesn't mean better" [D]. For search, matches in the content, hashtags and
  sound count [D]. A hook or a question that invites comments is said to work better than a plain caption [D].
- **Length:** about equal across all lengths for Reels, only very long texts do worse [B, weak, vendor data].
- **What really counts:** watch time, likes and shares; shares matter most for non-followers (statement by the head of
  Instagram, January 2025) [D, via trade press]. Caption and hashtags are secondary beside that.
- No #fyp, no #viral: there is no evidence for it [U].

## 3. Uploading

- Upload the original file natively in the app, never a video with the watermark of another app [D]. On Instagram, turn on
  "Upload at highest quality" [D].
- Instagram shows reels less often that are muted, blurry or largely covered by text [D].
- Cover: TikTok recommends 1080 × 1920 with text that summarises the content [D]. The Instagram profile grid shows the
  centre, so the title belongs in the middle [D].
- Best time: general tables are weak; use the "most active times" of your own followers in the statistics [U].
- Through the TikTok API you can only choose a cover frame by timestamp, not a custom cover image [D].

## 4. Music

- **Instagram:** Content with third-party music can be "blocked, muted or removed" if the use is not permitted; commercial
  use without a licence is prohibited [D]. Reels with music from the Instagram library or with original audio are
  recommended [D].
- **TikTok:** Posts can be removed, repeated violations lead to loss of the account [D]. Businesses should use the
  Commercial Music Library [D].
- **Safe route:** upload the version without music and add the song in the app from the platform's library, or use
  licensed music, or only voice and effects.

## 5. Trial reels, variants, posting again

- **Trial reels (Instagram "Trial Reels"):** for professional accounts; they are shown to non-followers first and do not
  appear in the profile grid until you choose "Share to everyone". Evaluation after about 24 hours; if you want, Instagram
  shares automatically when the reel does well in the first 72 hours [D]. The help page warns: "Your trial reel may get
  limited reach if we detect that you've previously shared the same content" [D]. Follower thresholds and daily limits are
  not documented [U].
- **Variants:** Trial Reels are the only official testing tool for organic content on Instagram [D]; none was found for
  TikTok [U]. Your own recordings count as original [D]; how similar two videos may be is stated nowhere [U].
- **Posting the same video again after a few weeks:** not documented as prohibited. Instagram lists already posted reels as
  shown less often [D, 2023]; TikTok does not recommend content again that someone has already seen [D]. There is no
  reliable data on the benefit [U]. Safer: a new export with a changed hook, a different cover and a different caption.
- **Small accounts:** at about 200 views per variant, a rate fluctuates by about ±7 percentage points; a real difference
  only shows from about 14 percentage points (the agent's own calculation). So change only one thing per round, first the
  first 1.5 seconds, and wait at least 72 hours.
- **Compare:** drop-off rate in the first 3 seconds, average watch time, shares per reach, new followers. Likes alone are
  too weak.
